The pattern behind all 18

Almost every AI product is one of six shapes: classify/extract (structured output), retrieve & answer (RAG), recommend/personalise (stats → ranking → LLM phrasing), summarise/generate, copilot (AI drafts, human decides), or agent (AI acts with tools). Name the shape first, and the architecture follows.

The universal build order

  1. Happy path with mock data and a stubbed AI response.
  2. Deterministic logic (rules, stats, filters) with tests.
  3. Real AI call: server-side, structured, validated, with fallback.
  4. Persistence + auth + RLS.
  5. Human-in-the-loop where actions are consequential.
  6. Metrics events, then polish.
