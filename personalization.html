Worked example: "Nudge", an AI personalization tracker

The full loop on one realistic prompt: scoping, spec, data model, architecture, the personalization logic, prompts for each build slice, verification, metrics and guardrails. Rebuild it yourself in 90 minutes, then in 45.

🎯 The prompt

"Build an AI personalization tracker that learns a user's habits and suggests one small daily improvement."

1 · Clarify & scope (minutes 0–7)

Questions I'd ask: What's the business goal: engagement, retention or monetisation? Mobile or web? Do we have any data (wearables, calendar) or start from zero? Any sensitivity constraints (health data)?

Assumed answers: goal = retention of new users; web-first (mobile-friendly); no integrations in v1; mood/sleep notes are sensitive.

SegmentPainFrequencyReach
Busy professionals restarting habitsStart strong, quit in week 2; no idea what actually helps themHighHigh (onboarding)
Students in exam seasonBurnout, inconsistent sleepSeasonalMedium
People managing a health conditionNeed to track triggersHighLow + regulated

Pick: busy professionals: biggest pain × most reachable × directly tied to the retention goal. The health segment is tempting but brings medical-advice risk into an MVP.

Solution (my call, not the AI's): a 20-second daily check-in, and after 7 days, one personalised micro-suggestion with a "why" drawn from the user's own data. Not a chatbot: users don't want another conversation, they want one useful nudge.

Why precompute nightly? Instant page loads, predictable cost (one call per active user per day), and the suggestion can't change on refresh. On-demand generation is the fallback for brand-new users who hit day 7 mid-day.

4 · The personalization logic (where PM judgement shows)

"Personalization" is a ladder. Climb only as far as the data and the stage justify:

LevelHowWhen
0 · Rules"Sleep < 6h for 3 days → suggest earlier wind-down."Day 1; cold start; fallback forever.
1 · Personal stats (MVP)Compare outcomes on days with vs without each habit/condition; rank by effect size and sample count.From ~7 check-ins.
2 · LLM phrasing (MVP)LLM turns the top pattern into a friendly, specific suggestion + why. It does not invent patterns.With level 1.
3 · Feedback learningDown-weight suggestion types the user dismisses; up-weight accepted ones.Week 2+.
4 · Bandits / population priorsContextual bandit chooses suggestion type; similar-user priors for cold start (embeddings).Thousands of users.

6 · Verify: the edge cases to show off

  • Day 1 user (no data) · exactly 7 check-ins · a user who always does every habit (no contrast → no "with vs without" pattern).
  • Two check-ins on the same day (should update, not duplicate) · timezone midnight.
  • LLM returns invalid JSON or medical advice → validation rejects → rule fallback.
  • Free-text note with an injection attempt ("ignore instructions…"): notes are never sent to the model.

7 · Metrics, risks, guardrails

Metric tree

North-star: % new users with ≥5 check-ins in first 10 days.
Inputs: D1 check-in completion · day-7 insight reach rate · suggestion accept rate · next-day return after accept.
Guardrails: dismiss rate · "creepy" feedback · cost per DAU · p95 latency.

Experiment

H: showing a personalised "why" increases next-day return vs a generic tip. A/B on new users, 2 weeks, primary metric = next-day check-in after a suggestion; MDE ~5 pp.

Risks

Spurious patterns from tiny samples · feels creepy · health-advice liability · cost creep · users gaming streaks.

Guardrails

Min sample sizes + "based on 9 days" in the why · transparency page and delete-all · no-medical-advice rule + validator · nightly batch with budget cap · snooze/dismiss learning.

The 45-minute interview version

Do slices 1, 3 (simplified), 4 and 5 with localStorage and a mocked LLM if needed. Spend the saved time on the "why from your own data" insight, the edge cases, and the metric tree. That's where you out-think the AI, which is the point of the round.

v2 roadmap to mention

  • Calendar/wearable integrations (with consent) · weekly reflection summary · contextual bandit for suggestion choice · similar-user priors for cold start · push notifications at the user's best time.
