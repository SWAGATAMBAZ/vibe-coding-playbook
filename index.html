🎯 The one idea that matters

Interviewers grade your judgement, not the AI's output. Coaches who've run these rounds describe four signals: the insight you add beyond the AI, using AI for routine work so you can spend time on trade-offs, turning ambiguity into structured instructions, and catching feasibility, privacy and policy problems the AI misses. Every page of this site trains one of those four signals.

What "vibe coding" means for a PM

For a PM, vibe coding means using an AI coding tool (Claude Code, Cursor, Replit) or an AI prototyping tool (Lovable, Bolt, v0) to turn a product idea into a working prototype fast. It does not mean you've become an engineer. It means you can:

  • Make the idea concrete: a clickable prototype beats a 10-page PRD in a review.
  • Direct the AI with a clear spec, constraints and acceptance criteria, instead of one vague request.
  • Verify what it built: run it, break it, read the diff, and catch hallucinated or insecure code.
  • Reason about the system: which database, where the LLM call lives, what it costs, what breaks at 10× users, what the privacy risk is.
PM lens

"I vibe coded it" is not a story. "I prototyped three onboarding flows in an afternoon, put them in front of 8 users, and killed two" is. Keep the product outcome at the centre of every demo and every interview answer.
